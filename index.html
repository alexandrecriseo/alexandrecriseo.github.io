Three apps I built for my own life.

I use them every day, or people close to me do. You can try each one here, in your browser.

Look Pilot

Tomorrow's office outfit, from the clothes you already own.

It picks tomorrow's outfit from my wardrobe, based on the weather and what is clean, and shows it on me. I can swap one piece and see the new look.

Built with Nano Banana 2 for the first render and Ideogram 4.5 to change one garment while keeping the face and pose.

Job Search Workbench

The few roles worth applying to, out of thousands.

The tool I run my job search with. It reads the public job boards of about 3,000 companies, drops the roles that miss my location or role type, and has an AI model score the rest from the full description.

Each score shows five dimensions, the reasons behind them and what to check before applying.

Open the workbench →
OuiChef

Recipe videos you saved, turned into meals you cook.

We save recipe videos and never cook them. Paste an Instagram, TikTok or YouTube link and OuiChef writes out the recipe, then helps plan the week and builds the shopping list.

Scaling a recipe for more guests changes the amounts and leaves cooking times alone.

About me

I'm a product leader based in Paris. Since January 2026 I have been CPO at Ekie, a French legal assistance platform offered by 1,000 works councils to 500,000 employees, where I own the member app, the lawyer workspace and the back office.
