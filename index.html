Job Search Workbench

From a noisy job feed to a deliberate application decision.

The tool I run my own job search with. It pulls roles from public applicant-tracking APIs, drops the ones that fail hard criteria, then drafts a score that I validate by hand.

  • Explain before automating. Every score shows its five dimensions, its reasons and the points still to check, so the decision stays with me.
  • Separate priority from progress. Filters decide what to review; stages record a decision.
  • One role, one line. A role published in several countries is merged into a single card.

OuiChef

From saved food inspiration to a meal you actually cook.

We save recipes from videos and posts, then never cook them: quantities are vague, nothing is planned and the shopping list is missing. OuiChef connects the loop: save, review, plan, shop, cook.

  • Review before trust. An imported recipe starts as an editable draft, because AI extraction can get quantities wrong.
  • One quantity engine. Portions, meal planning and the grocery list share the same rules. Scaling a recipe changes amounts and leaves times and temperatures alone.
  • Local first. The library, plan and checklists work without an account.

Look Pilot

Tomorrow's office outfit, from the clothes you already own.

Most mornings the hard part is picking a combination that suits the weather, the day and what is clean. Look Pilot suggests one outfit, shows it on you, and lets you swap a single piece.

  • Edit one garment, keep the person. Changing a piece edits only that garment on the last photo with Ideogram 4.5, so the face and pose stay the same.
  • Show the wait. A swap takes about 9 seconds while trying combinations; the app shows the progress instead of hiding it.

About me

Before that I spent five years at Fintecture, a payments fintech, as its first Product Manager, then Lead PM, then Product Director. Over that period the company went from 100 to more than 250,000 payments a month. Smart Transfer, the product I took over in 2022, reached €100M a month within 12 months.

Outside work I build the software I need. The products on this page solve problems from my own life, and I keep using them.
