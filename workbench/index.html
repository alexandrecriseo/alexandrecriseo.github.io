Job Search Workbench

The tool I run my job search with. It reads the public job boards of about 3,000 companies, drops the roles that miss my location or role type, and has an AI model score the rest from the full description.

Try it: open a role to compare its two scores, then change its stage.

Two scores per role

  • ~ HeuristicInstant keyword rules on the title, location and description. Every new role gets one.
  • ✦ AIA model reads the whole description against my background and scores five dimensions, with reasons. It runs only on roles that pass my criteria.
